Frequently Asked Questions

What should be brought to the first visit?

Carry a current medication list, recent lab reports, imaging (if available), and any previous discharge summaries. For diabetes or high blood pressure, bringing recent readings can be helpful.

When should a nephrologist be consulted?

Consider a consultation for persistent swelling, uncontrolled blood pressure, abnormal urine reports, reduced kidney function in labs, or kidney‑related symptoms advised by a physician. This page is educational and does not replace medical advice.

Is education and counseling part of the care?

Yes. Time is spent explaining the condition and the plan clearly so that patients and families can make informed decisions.
