Common Questions

What should I bring to my first visit? +

Please bring your current medication list, recent lab reports, imaging results (CT/USG), and any discharge summaries. Recent blood pressure and blood sugar readings are also very helpful.

When should I see a nephrologist? +

Consider a consultation if you have persistent leg swelling, high blood pressure that is hard to control, abnormal urine tests (protein/blood), or if your blood tests show reduced kidney function (high creatinine).

Do you offer online consultations? +

Initial consultations are best done in person for a thorough examination. Follow-ups can sometimes be managed remotely depending on the condition.
