Understanding Your Kidney Tests

eGFR, creatinine, urea, ACR — decode the numbers on your lab report and know what they mean for you.

Home/Education/Understanding Your Kidney Tests

Kidney health is assessed with two simple, inexpensive tests: a blood test that estimates how well your kidneys filter, and a urine test that checks whether they are leaking protein. Together they tell your doctor the stage and cause of any kidney problem.

eGFR — your kidney "filtering score"

The estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate (eGFR) estimates how many millilitres of blood your kidneys clean each minute. It is calculated from your blood creatinine, age and sex. A higher number is better.

eGFRWhat it suggests
90+Normal kidney filtering
60–89Mildly reduced (normal if no other signs)
30–59Moderately reduced function
15–29Severely reduced function
Below 15Kidney failure

Serum creatinine

Creatinine is a waste product from muscle activity that healthy kidneys remove. When kidney function falls, creatinine rises in the blood. Because it depends on muscle mass, creatinine is always interpreted alongside eGFR rather than on its own.

Blood urea (BUN)

Urea is another waste product filtered by the kidneys. It can rise with reduced kidney function, but also with dehydration or a high-protein diet, so it is read in context.

Urine ACR — checking for protein leak

The Albumin-to-Creatinine Ratio (ACR) measures albumin (a protein) in a urine sample. Healthy kidneys keep protein in the blood; a leak is one of the earliest signs of kidney damage.

Urine ACR (mg/g)Meaning
Below 30Normal
30–300Moderately increased (early damage)
Above 300Severely increased

The two numbers that matter most

Modern kidney care stages disease using both eGFR and ACR together. Knowing both gives a far more accurate picture than either one alone.

How often should I be tested?

If you have diabetes, high blood pressure, heart disease, or a family history of kidney failure, an annual eGFR and urine ACR is generally recommended. Your doctor may test more often if results are abnormal.
